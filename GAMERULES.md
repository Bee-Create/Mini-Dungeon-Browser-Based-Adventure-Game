<h1>🗡️ Mini Dungeon — Game Rules</h1>

Welcome, adventurer! Mini Dungeon is a small, browser-based fantasy adventure where you explore a mysterious dungeon, battle monsters, collect treasure, and face a final boss.
<br>
Your goal is simple: survive all eight rooms and defeat the Dungeon Overlord.
<br>
<h2>🎮 Getting Started</h2>
<ul>
<li>You begin with 30 health points (HP).</li>
<li>You start with 3 healing potions.</li>
<li>The dungeon contains 8 rooms.</li>
<li>Each room can bring a different encounter, so be prepared for the unexpected!</li>
</ul>
<br>
Your health matters. If it reaches 0 HP, your adventure ends in defeat.
<br>
<h2>🧭 Available Actions</h2>

<b>Explore</b><br>
Advance through the dungeon and discover what awaits you. Exploring can trigger a monster encounter, uncover treasure, reveal a potion cache, or lead to a quiet room where you can recover some health.
<br>
<b>Attack</b><br>
Fight your current enemy! Each attack deals a random 4–8 damage. If the enemy survives, it will counterattack.
<br>
<b>Use Potion</b><br>
Consume one potion to restore 12 HP, up to your maximum of 30 HP.<br>
Potions are limited, so use them wisely. During combat, using a potion gives your enemy an opportunity to counterattack.
<br>
<b>Retreat</b><br>
Escape from an ordinary monster encounter when a fight becomes too dangerous.<br>
Warning: You cannot retreat from the final boss battle!
<br>
<b>Play Again</b><br>
Start a fresh adventure and try your luck again.
<br>
<h2>❤️ Health and Combat</h2>

Your health is your most important resource.
<ul>
<li>You begin with 30 HP.</li>
<li>Enemies deal damage when they counterattack.</li>
<li>Enemy attacks vary, so combat can be unpredictable.</li>
<li>You can recover health using potions or by discovering certain rooms.</li>
<li>If your health reaches zero, you lose the adventure.</li>
</ul>
<br>
Enemy counterattacks can deal their base damage plus an additional random 0–2 damage.

<h2>🧪 Healing Potions</h2>
Potions restore 12 HP per use, but they cannot raise your health above 30 HP.<br>
You begin with three potions, and additional potions may be discovered while exploring.<br>
Save your supplies for dangerous encounters, especially when your health is low.
<br>

<h3>The Final Boss: Dungeon Overlord</h3>
At the end of your journey, you must face the Dungeon Overlord.
<br>
HP = 28
Base Damage = 7
Gold Reward = 50
<br>
The Overlord is your <strong>final</strong> challenge. You must defeat this boss to win the game, and retreat is not an option.
<br>
<h2>💰 Treasure and Rewards</h2>
Exploration may reward you with treasure and gold. Defeating enemies also earns gold, with stronger opponents offering greater rewards.<br>
Keep exploring, survive your encounters, and see how much treasure you can collect before the adventure ends.
<br>
<h2>🏆 Winning and Losing</h2>
<em>You win when</em>: You defeat the Dungeon Overlord and survive the final battle.<br>
<em>You lose when</em>: Your health reaches zero.<br>
There is no shame in a failed adventure. Learn from your mistakes, manage your resources, and try again!
<br>
<h2>⚔️ Final Advice</h2>
<ul>
<li>Keep an eye on your health.=</li>
<li>Don't waste potions when you're already near full health.</li>
<li>Remember that enemies can strike back after you attack or use a potion during combat.</li>
<li>Retreat from an ordinary fight if survival is more important than the reward.</li>
<li>Prepare yourself before entering the final room.</li>
</ul>
<br>
<b>Good luck, adventurer. The dungeon awaits!</b>
